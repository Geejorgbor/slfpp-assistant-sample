1 The problem

When people come into the program, loans move, and payment days arrive, staff need one clear place to look — not scattered notebooks, chats, or memory.

“So-and-so should pay today” should never be a surprise. Incoming people should never go unregistered. Loan history per person should be instant.

2 What you get

1 · Incoming registration

Clear registration of people coming in — name, phone, community, purpose, officer, status — plus a live list of recent intakes.

2 · Loan track record

Per person: loans opened, principal, balance remaining, and a full payments timeline (disbursements + installments).

3 · Reminders

Notifications that highlight who should pay today (and who is overdue), with status badges and one-tap “send reminder” actions.

3 How reminders work

  • Due today list — each morning, staff see members whose installment falls on that date (e.g. “Mary Kollie should pay today”).
  • Amounts & loan IDs — expected LRD amount and loan number sit next to the name so follow-up is fast.
  • Overdue flags — missed payments surface with a clear overdue badge before they stack up.
  • Queue reminders — mark reminders for WhatsApp / SMS follow-up; mark paid when cash or mobile money lands.
